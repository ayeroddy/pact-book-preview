You gave them space. You sent the card. You went to the counselor.

Almost every parent who talks to Catherine's team has already tried three things.

They went to a counselor who listened, kindly, for an hour at a time, and never handed them a plan. They wrote the letter, or the text, and watched it go unanswered, or come back with one word. And they gave their child space, because everyone said to, and the space grew into a year, and the year grew into a silence that now has a shape at the holiday table.

None of those were wrong. A good counselor matters. Space is sometimes exactly right. The letter may have been the kindest thing you've ever written.

They just weren't a plan. And the holidays are the one time of year when not having a plan costs the most, because every loaded date arrives on schedule, whether you're ready or not.

It starts with you, and it can start this week.

Catherine has sat with families for forty years as a licensed psychotherapist, and the first thing she teaches is that estrangement isn't one thing. It runs in six patterns, and the same message that opens a door in one pattern closes it in another.

1
The Slow FadeNothing broke. Contact just thinned, year by year, until it went quiet.
2
The RuptureA single event, a fight or a confrontation, and then the door shut.
3
The RewriteYour child remembers a childhood you don't recognize.
4
The New HouseholdA spouse or partner now shapes how much of your child you get.
5
The Shielded DoorSomeone on their side of the door is holding it closed.
6
The StandoffBoth sides are waiting for the other to go first.

That is why "just send a warm note at Christmas" helps one family and hurts another. In a Slow Fade, a brief card is a gift. In a Standoff, the same card can read as a move in a game your child has decided not to play. Knowing which family you are in is the difference between doing the right thing and doing the right-sounding thing.

And the first move is yours. Not because you caused the distance, but because you are the one reading this, and it only takes one person to change what happens next. The Holiday Edition gives you the map of the six patterns, the exact words for each loaded date, and a conversation with Catherine's team to put a plan under your season.

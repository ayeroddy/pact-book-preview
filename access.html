One thing that trips people up

Your book lives in your online library — it opens right in your web browser. It isn't a file you save to your phone.

If you once tried to save it and it wouldn't open afterwards, that's why. Your phone saved the page, not the book. Nothing is wrong with your device.

Once your library opens on your phone, tap the share button, then "Add to Home Screen." It becomes an icon, just like an app — one tap, every time.

And if you'd rather keep your own copy, there's a "Download the book (PDF)" button waiting inside your library.

The curriculum

1
The Wrong Diagnosis

Estrangement is not a communication problem — it’s a safety problem. Why every tool you were handed rests on an assumption that feels true and isn’t.

2
Why the Standard Advice Fails

The safety account: deposits, withdrawals, and the overdrawn branch. Why the perfect letter doesn’t exist, why “give them space” hardens silence, and the overreach reflex.

3
What Therapy Can’t Reach

The person who most needs to feel the change isn’t in the room; conversation tools can’t fix an absent conversation; and why the right next step differs across the six patterns.

4
The Path That Actually Works

Diagnosis first, then safety, then words. The P.A.R.E.N.T. sequence — and why the first three stages happen entirely on your side of the silence.

Tonight’s assignment
  1. Put the phone down. For the next 14 days the plan doesn’t involve sending your child anything. If the reflex flares, write it in your journal instead.
  2. Take the Estrangement Decoder in your library — twenty minutes to name which of the six patterns your family is in.
  3. Open Chapter Two, “Don’t Reach Out Yet.” Day one of your first fourteen days is tonight.
